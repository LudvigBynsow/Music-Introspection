Welcome to Music Introspective!

This is a game where you have to dive deep into your own music listening habits and guess which track out of two that you have listened to more.

To play, simply press the "Start Introspection" button on the main screen and take a look at the tracks that pop up.

If you think you have listened to one more than the other, select it!

Beat your own high score and compete with friends to see who knows their music-self best!
